# LV8R LABS

Downloads for **LV8R LABS** audio plugins and **Control Room**, the app that installs, activates and updates them.

**Get Control Room for Mac:** [Control-Room-mac.dmg](https://github.com/lv8r777/lv8r-labs/releases/download/updates/Control-Room-mac.dmg)

### First time opening Control Room
Control Room isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks once:
1. Open the DMG and drag **Control Room** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. When macOS says it can't verify the developer, click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Control Room. Enter your password.

That's it, once. Plugins installed through Control Room open in your DAW with no extra steps, and Control Room updates itself from here.
